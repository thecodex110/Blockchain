The course splits the project into three worklets + integration. Back to [[Home]].

| Worklet | Theme | Subjects | Main files | Concepts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Cryptography & transactions | Discrete maths, OOP | `number_theory.py`, `asymmetric.py`, `transaction.py`, `wallet.py` | [[Number Theory and RSA]], [[Transactions]] |
